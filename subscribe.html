Live team calendar subscriptions

Subscribe once to see schedule changes as your calendar app refreshes the feed. Updates aren't necessarily immediate.

🗓️

Google Calendar

Google Calendar requires adding a calendar by URL on its website (not directly in the mobile app).

Open Google Calendar
How to subscribe
  1. Copy the live calendar URL below.
  2. Open Google Calendar on the web and choose Other calendars → + → From URL.
  3. Paste the URL and select Add calendar.
🍎

Apple Calendar

Open the subscription link on an Apple device, or add the URL through Calendar's subscription options.

Subscribe with Apple Calendar
Manual instructions
  1. Copy the live calendar URL below.
  2. On iPhone, open Calendar, tap Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar.
  3. Paste the URL and follow the prompts.
✉️

Outlook

Add the team feed using Outlook on the web.

Open Outlook Calendar
How to subscribe
  1. Copy the live calendar URL below.
  2. In Outlook Calendar, select Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the URL, name your calendar and select Import.

Use this URL when your calendar app asks for an internet calendar or subscription feed.

Download vs subscribe: Downloading an .ics file imports a one-time copy. Subscribing by URL lets your calendar app check for schedule updates. The refresh frequency is controlled by your calendar provider.
