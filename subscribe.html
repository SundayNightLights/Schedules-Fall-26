Team Calendar Subscription

Subscribe using your calendar app to receive schedule updates.

Google Calendar

  1. Copy your team's subscription URL below.
  2. Open Google Calendar in a browser (desktop view may be required).
  3. Under Other calendars, choose From URL, paste the link and select Add calendar.

Apple Calendar

In Calendar, choose Add Subscription Calendar and paste the subscription URL. Do not use Import.

Outlook

In Outlook Calendar, choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web, then paste the URL.
